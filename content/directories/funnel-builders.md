# Best Funnel Builders (2026): 14 Sales Funnel Platforms Compared

A landing page captures a lead. A sales funnel captures a lead, nurtures it, presents an offer, handles the checkout, triggers an upsell, and follows up — all automatically. Funnel builders are the all-in-one evolution of landing page builders: they bundle page building, email automation, payment processing, order bumps, one-click upsells, and affiliate management into a single platform. For conversion-focused marketers, the funnel builder is the revenue engine of the entire stack.

The market has segmented into distinct camps. There are course creator platforms optimized for digital products and memberships (Kajabi, Kartra, Podia). There are agency platforms built for managing multiple client sub-accounts (GoHighLevel, Keap). There are budget all-in-ones that undercut everyone on price (Systeme.io, Builderall). There are checkout-first platforms that specialize in high-converting payment flows (SamCart, ThriveCart). And there are ecommerce-specific funnel builders for physical product brands (CartFlows, Funnelish, Convertri). Pricing ranges from free to $500+ per month, with wildly different value propositions at each tier.

This page compares 14 sales funnel builders that conversion-focused marketers actually use in 2026. Every price was verified against the vendor's current pricing page as of September 2026. ## How We Evaluated

We assessed each funnel builder across six criteria that matter to conversion-focused marketers:

1. **Funnel architecture and flow builder** — Can you build multi-step funnels with order bumps, one-click upsells, downsells, and cross-sells? Does it support webinar funnels, launch funnels, and membership funnels? Can you visualize the entire flow before building it? The depth of funnel architecture is the single biggest differentiator between a basic page builder and a true funnel platform.

2. **Checkout and payment processing** — Does it handle checkout pages natively, or do you need a separate tool? Does it support multiple payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal, Apple Pay)? Are order bumps, one-click upsells, and subscription management built in? The checkout is where the conversion actually happens — a funnel builder with a weak checkout is just an expensive page builder.

3. **Pricing transparency and scalability** — How does the price change as your business grows? Some tools that look cheap at 500 contacts become expensive at 10,000. We verified every price against the vendor's live pricing page as of September 2026. We flagged tools with hidden contact caps, transaction fees, and platform fees.

4. **Email automation and CRM** — Does it include email marketing, or do you need a separate platform? Can you trigger email sequences based on funnel behavior (purchase, abandonment, upsell acceptance)? Is there a built-in CRM for managing contacts and deals? A true all-in-one funnel platform replaces your email tool, your CRM, and your page builder.

5. **Course and membership hosting** — Can it host digital products, online courses, and membership areas? Does it support drip content, video hosting, and community features? For course creators and coaches, this is often the deciding factor between a funnel builder and a standalone platform.

6. **Affiliate program availability** — Noted but not scored. Several platforms offer recurring commissions (30-70%), which matters if you recommend tools to clients or readers. We tag these with [AFFILIATE-LINK] for our affiliate team to pursue.

We did **not** score based on brand recognition, VC funding rounds, or feature count checklists. We scored based on whether the tool helps you build funnels that capture leads, process payments, and maximize revenue per customer.

### 1. ClickFunnels

ClickFunnels is the platform that defined the sales funnel category. Created by Russell Brunson, it popularized the concept of multi-step funnels with order bumps, upsells, and downsells as a structured revenue optimization system. The platform's biggest strength is its ecosystem — the ClickFunnels community, training materials, and funnel templates (Funnel Hacks) are unmatched in depth and quality. If you want to learn funnel marketing as a discipline, ClickFunnels is the platform that teaches it. The Startup plan at $97/mo includes unlimited funnels, landing pages, and courses, making it accessible for most creators.

**Pricing (verified September 2026):**
- **Startup:** $97/mo (or $81/mo billed annually) — unlimited funnels, landing pages, courses, 3 custom domains, basic email automation
- **Scale:** $197/mo — more domains, advanced automation, affiliate management
- **Optimize/Pro:** $297/mo (or $248/mo billed annually) — unlimited everything, advanced features, priority support
- 14-day free trial

**Pros:**
- Defined the sales funnel category — best funnel templates and training ecosystem
- Unlimited funnels and pages on every plan
- Built-in email automation, course hosting, and affiliate management
- Strong community and educational resources (Funnel Hacks, One Funnel Away)
- Russell Brunson's marketing training is genuinely valuable for beginners

**Cons:**
- $97/mo is expensive compared to Systeme.io ($17/mo) or Builderall ($27/mo)
- Email automation is basic compared to dedicated platforms like ActiveCampaign
- Page builder is less flexible than dedicated tools like Unbounce or Webflow
- No free plan — 14-day trial only
- Pro plan at $297/mo is steep for small creators

**Best for:** Entrepreneurs, course creators, and coaches who want the original funnel platform with the best training ecosystem and template library.

## How to Choose a Funnel Builder

Choosing the right funnel builder depends on what you're selling and who you're selling it to. Here's a practical framework based on use case:

**If you're a course creator or coach:**
You need course hosting, membership areas, and email automation alongside funnels. **Kajabi** ($179/mo) is the premium choice with the best student experience. **Kartra** ($99/mo) is the all-in-one alternative with more features at a lower price. **ClickFunnels** ($97/mo) is the original with the best training ecosystem. **Systeme.io** ($17/mo) is the budget choice with everything included.

**If you're an agency managing multiple clients:**
**GoHighLevel** ($97/mo Starter, $297/mo Unlimited) is purpose-built for agencies with sub-accounts, white-labeling, and client billing. No other platform comes close for agency operations.

**If you sell digital products (not courses):**
**SamCart** ($79/mo) has the best checkout optimization with order bumps and one-click upsells. **ThriveCart** ($495 one-time) offers lifetime pricing with no monthly fees. **Podia** ($42/mo) is the simplest option for basic digital downloads.

**If you sell physical products (ecommerce):**
**CartFlows** (free → $399/yr) is the best for WordPress/WooCommerce stores. **Funnelish** ($49/mo) is the best for Shopify/DTC brands. **Convertri** ($99/mo) is the best for speed-optimized PPC funnels.

**If you're on a tight budget:**
**Systeme.io** (free → $17/mo) is unbeatable for an all-in-one platform. **CartFlows** (free) is unbeatable for WordPress users. **Builderall** ($27/mo) offers the most tools per dollar.

**If you're a service business (not a creator):**
**Keap** ($249/mo) is the CRM-first platform with the best automation engine for service businesses that need scheduling, invoicing, and pipeline management alongside funnels.

## Funnel Builder Pricing Trends (2026)

1. **All-in-one platforms are consolidating the market** — ClickFunnels, Kartra, GoHighLevel, and Systeme.io all bundle funnels, email, courses, and CRM into a single subscription, reducing the need for separate tools.
2. **Budget platforms are disrupting pricing** — Systeme.io ($17/mo) and Builderall ($27/mo) offer comparable feature breadth to ClickFunnels ($97/mo) at a fraction of the cost, pressuring the market downward.
3. **Checkout-first platforms are diverging** — SamCart's revenue-based pricing and ThriveCart's one-time pricing model represent fundamentally different approaches from the subscription model that dominates the category.
4. **WordPress-native tools are the value play** — CartFlows (free → $399/yr) offers enterprise-grade funnel features on WordPress with no monthly SaaS fees, making it the most cost-effective option for WordPress users.
5. **Ecommerce-specific funnel builders are emerging** — Funnelish and Convertri are building purpose-built funnel platforms for physical product brands, a segment that generic funnel builders like ClickFunnels don't serve well.
