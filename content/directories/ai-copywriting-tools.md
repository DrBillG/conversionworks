## How We Evaluated

We assessed each tool across six criteria that matter to conversion-focused marketers:

1. **Output quality** — Does the AI produce copy that a human would actually publish? We tested each tool on the same prompts: a landing page headline, a Facebook ad, a product description, and a blog intro. We judged for clarity, persuasiveness, and tone.
2. **Pricing transparency** — Is the pricing page clear? Are there hidden usage caps? We list the real starting price, not the "starting at $1" teaser.
3. **Feature depth** — Does the tool do one thing well (like Rytr for quick copy) or many things competently (like Jasper for full campaigns)? Neither is inherently better — it depends on your use case.
4. **Ease of use** — Can a non-technical marketer open the tool and produce usable copy within 10 minutes? Or does it require a steep learning curve?
5. **Template & use-case variety** — Does the tool offer templates for the copy types you actually write? We weighted tools that cover ads, landing pages, emails, and product descriptions.
6. **Affiliate availability** — Noted but not scored. Some tools have generous affiliate programs (30–45% recurring); others have none. This matters for cost recovery if you're recommending tools to clients.

We did **not** score based on hype, Twitter mentions, or VC funding. We scored based on whether the tool helps you write better copy faster.

## The 15 Best AI Copywriting Tools

### 1. Jasper

Jasper is the most recognized name in AI copywriting, built specifically for marketing teams rather than general-purpose use. It includes a Brand Voice feature that learns your company's tone, templates for 50+ copy types, and workflow automation for multi-step campaigns. Jasper Boss Mode (now integrated into the Business plan) connects to web search so the AI can pull current data into its outputs.

**Pricing:**
- **Free trial:** 7 days
- **Creator:** $39/month — 1 brand voice, 50+ templates, 1 user
- **Pro:** $59/month — 5 brand voices, 3 users, SEO mode, campaigns
- **Business:** Custom pricing — unlimited brand voices, API access, SSO

**Pros:**
- Best-in-class Brand Voice consistency across outputs
- Robust template library for ads, emails, landing pages, and blogs
- Campaign feature chains multiple assets together automatically
- Strong integration ecosystem (Surfer SEO, Chrome extension)

**Cons:**
- Most expensive option on this list at the Pro tier
- Word count limits apply even on paid plans (Creative Boost excluded)
- Learning curve for teams new to AI workflows

🔗 [Visit Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai) — [AFFILIATE-LINK]

### 2. Copy.ai

Copy.ai positions itself as the go-to platform for startups and solo marketers who need marketing copy without the enterprise price tag. It offers 90+ templates covering everything from Instagram captions to cold email sequences, plus a workflow builder that automates multi-step content tasks. The free tier is genuinely usable — 2,000 words per month with access to most templates.

**Pricing:**
- **Free:** 2,000 words/month, 1 seat, basic templates
- **Starter:** $36/month — 45,000 words/month, 1 seat, 90+ templates, 5 workflows
- **Advanced:** $186/month — 5 seats, unlimited words, custom workflows, API
- **Enterprise:** Custom — SSO, security review, dedicated support

**Pros:**
- Most generous free tier in the category (2,000 words/mo with real template access)
- 90+ templates cover virtually every marketing copy type
- Workflow builder lets you automate content production pipelines
- 45% affiliate commission — one of the highest in SaaS

**Cons:**
- Output quality can be inconsistent on complex long-form content
- Brand voice features are limited to higher tiers
- Some templates produce generic copy without careful prompt engineering

🔗 [Visit Copy.ai](https://www.copy.ai) — [AFFILIATE-LINK]

### 4. ChatGPT (OpenAI)

ChatGPT isn't a dedicated copywriting tool — it's a general-purpose AI assistant that happens to be exceptional at writing copy. The conversational interface lets you iterate on copy in real time, and GPT-4o produces output quality that rivals or beats every specialized tool on this list. The catch: you do all the prompt engineering yourself, and there are no templates, brand voice features, or workflow automations.

**Pricing:**
- **Free:** GPT-4o mini, limited messages
- **Plus:** $20/month — GPT-4o, 80 messages/3 hours, DALL-E, Code Interpreter
- **Team:** $25/month/user — higher limits, shared workspace
- **Enterprise:** Custom — unlimited, SSO, data privacy guarantees

**Pros:**
- Output quality is best-in-class for general-purpose writing
- Conversational interface enables iterative refinement — ask for changes in plain English
- Extremely versatile: copywriting, brainstorming, editing, coding, analysis
- The $20/month Plus plan may be all most freelancers need

**Cons:**
- No templates, no brand voice training, no content workflows
- You must craft your own prompts — there's no "generate ad copy" button
- No built-in SEO optimization or keyword integration
- Word/usage caps on the Plus plan can be restrictive for high-volume users

🔗 [Visit ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com)

- **Data-Driven:** $99/month — unlimited words, custom audience targeting, 3 seats
- **Enterprise:** Custom — API, advanced analytics, SSO

**Pros:**
- Predictive performance scoring is genuinely unique in this category
- Audience targeting lets you tailor copy to specific demographics and channels
- Strong for ad copy specifically (Facebook, Google, LinkedIn)
- A/B testing integration available on higher tiers

**Cons:**

The browser extension lets you highlight any sentence and get 5–10 alternative phrasings instantly. Wordtune also includes a summarizer, a tone shifter (casual → formal and vice versa), and a "spice it up" feature that adds specificity to vague sentences.

**Pricing:**
- **Free:** 10 rewrites/day, 3 tone options
- **Plus:** $9.99/month — unlimited rewrites, 12+ tones, summarizer, spice feature
- **Business:** Custom — team licenses, style enforcement, admin controls

**Pros:**
- Best tool for editing and refining existing copy (not just generating new text)
- Browser extension works in Google Docs, Gmail, LinkedIn, WordPress — anywhere
- Tone shifting is genuinely useful for adapting copy across audiences
- Affordable at $9.99/month

**Cons:**
- Not a standalone copywriting tool — you need existing text to edit
- No templates, no long-form generation, no SEO features
- Limited to sentence-level edits; can't restructure paragraphs or full pages
- No affiliate program (as of this writing)

🔗 [Visit Wordtune](https://www.wordtune.com)

## How to Choose the Right AI Copywriting Tool

There's no single best tool — there's the best tool **for your situation**. Here's how to decide:

### If you're a solo marketer or freelancer
**Get Copy.ai (free tier) + ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo).** Copy.ai's templates give you structure for standard copy types; ChatGPT gives you unlimited flexibility for custom work. Total: $20/month.

### If you're an enterprise marketing team
**Get Jasper (Pro, $59/mo) or Writer.com (Team, $58/mo/user).** Jasper if you need campaign workflows and template variety; Writer.com if brand consistency across a large team is your top priority.

### If you're focused on SEO content
**Get Frase ($14.99/mo) or Scalenut ($17.99/mo).** Both give you SERP research + AI writing in one workflow. Frase if you prefer content briefs; Scalenut if you want NLP grading and optimization.

### If you're an eCommerce store
**Get Copysmith ($19/mo) or Hypotenuse AI ($29/mo).** Copysmith if you need bulk product descriptions with Shopify integration; Hypotenuse if you want images alongside your copy.

### If you're on a tight budget
**Get Rytr ($9/mo).** Unlimited characters, fast output, 30+ use cases. It won't win awards for sophistication, but it will get you usable copy for less than the cost of lunch.

### If you need ad copy variants for A/B testing
**Get Peppertype.ai ($35/mo) or Anyword ($49/mo).** Peppertype for speed and volume; Anyword if you want predictive performance scoring to prioritize which variants to test first.
