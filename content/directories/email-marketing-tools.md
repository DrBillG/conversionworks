## How We Evaluated

We assessed each platform across six criteria that matter to conversion-focused marketers:

1. **Automation capabilities** — Can you build multi-step workflows, trigger emails based on user behavior, and set up abandoned cart sequences? This is the single biggest differentiator between basic newsletter tools and serious marketing platforms.
2. **Pricing transparency and scalability** — How does the price change as your list grows? Some tools that look cheap at 500 contacts become expensive at 10,000. We verified every price against the vendor's live pricing page as of September 2026.
3. **Deliverability** — Does the platform maintain strong sender reputation and inbox placement? Tools with shared IP pools on free tiers typically underperform here.
4. **Ecommerce integration** — Does it integrate with Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, and other platforms? Can it track revenue attributed to email campaigns?
5. **Ease of use** — How steep is the learning curve? Can a non-technical marketer build a campaign and automation without watching 20 tutorials?
6. **Affiliate program availability** — Noted but not scored. Several platforms offer recurring commissions (20-60%), which matters if you recommend tools to clients or readers. We tag these with [AFFILIATE-LINK] for our affiliate team to pursue.

We did **not** score based on brand recognition, VC funding rounds, or feature count checklists. We scored based on whether the tool helps you send emails that reach the inbox, get opened, and drive conversions.

## Pricing Model Comparison: Which Model Saves You Money?

The biggest hidden cost in email marketing isn't the sticker price — it's the pricing model. Three models dominate the industry, and the right one depends on your list composition and sending frequency.

### Per-Contact Pricing (Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, Klaviyo, GetResponse, Campaign Monitor)

You pay based on the number of contacts stored in your account, regardless of whether you email them. This is the most common model. It works well if you have a clean, engaged list. It penalizes you for keeping dormant subscribers.

**Best for:** Businesses with smaller, highly engaged lists who send frequently.

### Per-Email-Volume Pricing (Brevo, Mailjet, Selzy)

You pay based on how many emails you send, not how many contacts you store. Unlimited contacts on every plan. This model is dramatically cheaper for businesses with large lists but lower send frequency.

**Best for:** Businesses with large contact lists who send less frequently (monthly newsletters, occasional promotions).

### Per-Subscriber Pricing (Kit, Drip)

Similar to per-contact, but typically counts only active subscribers (those who can receive emails). Some platforms, like Kit, offer generous free tiers up to 10,000 subscribers.

**Best for:** Creators and content publishers who want predictable pricing tied to actual audience size.

## How to Choose an Email Marketing Tool

### 1. Start With Your Business Model

Ecommerce stores should prioritize Klaviyo or Omnisend — both track revenue attribution and integrate deeply with Shopify. Creators and newsletter writers should look at Kit for its creator-specific features and generous free plan. B2B companies should consider ActiveCampaign for its CRM and automation depth.

### 2. Calculate Real Cost at Your Expected List Size

Don't compare platforms at 500 contacts — compare them at your expected list size 12 months from now. At 10,000 contacts, Mailchimp costs ~$100+/month while MailerLite costs ~$73/month and Brevo costs ~$25/month for the same list. The pricing model (per-contact vs per-email) matters more than the entry price.

### 3. Test the Automation Builder

Most platforms offer a free trial or free plan. Before committing, build one automation workflow — a welcome sequence with 3-4 emails, conditional branching based on engagement, and a tag application. If the builder feels intuitive, the platform is a fit. If you're fighting the interface, move on.

### 4. Check Integration Compatibility

Verify that the platform integrates with your existing tools — CRM, ecommerce platform, landing page builder, analytics. The most feature-rich platform is useless if it doesn't connect to your tech stack.

### 5. Consider Migration Cost

If you're already using a platform, factor in migration time and data transfer. Most platforms offer migration tools or services, but contact lists, automation workflows, and templates don't always transfer cleanly. Kit and Brevo offer free migration assistance on paid plans.
