1. Who we are

The Content Creator Machine is provided by Ministry Dynamics LLC, doing business as MinistryAI ("we," "us," "our"), 777 Loma Vista Dr, Redding, CA 96002, United States. For the personal information described here, we are the controller: we decide why and how it is processed.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Website addresses and chat messages. The URLs you submit, and anything you type in the chat, such as business details, notes, and revision requests.
  • Account information. When you sign up, your name and email address. Accounts are created and managed through our chat provider, MeclabsAI.
  • Billing information. Payments are handled by Stripe. We receive your plan, payment status, amount, and billing email. We do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Messages to us. What you send when you email us.

Information created or collected when you use the service

  • Content the tool reads. To research your business, the tool reads public web pages, including your site and competitors' sites. Those pages can contain names or contact details that their owners published.
  • Your outputs. The research, article, AEO pack, social posts, and revisions the tool creates for you.
  • Usage and device data. If you allow the chat, it records how it is used, such as conversations started, messages sent, and which tool ran. It also records technical data such as IP address, browser type, and the page where the chat opened. Our website host keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser, and time of visit.
  • Your privacy choice. Whether you allowed the chat, stored in your own browser.

We do not ask for sensitive personal information, such as health, religious beliefs, or government ID numbers. Please do not enter it in the chat.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run the Content Creator Machine, and only as needed for the purposes above.

  • MeclabsAI (MECLABS). Hosts the chat, user accounts, the AI tools, and the chat's usage analytics.
  • AI model providers, currently OpenAI and Google. They generate the research, articles, and posts from your inputs. Our company-wide notice also lists Amazon Web Services AI for other MinistryAI products.
  • Web search. The research tools send search queries, such as your business name, category, and topics, to a web search service used by MeclabsAI, and they read the public pages found.
  • Stripe. Processes subscription payments.
  • Our website host. Serves this website and keeps standard server logs.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. How the AI works with your data

  • The chat is an AI system. What it writes is an AI-generated draft for you to review before you publish.
  • Your inputs and outputs are processed by the AI model providers listed above to generate your pack.
  • We do not use your inputs or outputs to train AI models.
  • The tool is built to use facts from your own site or from what you tell it, and to mark anything it cannot verify for your review. It can still make mistakes, so please check before publishing.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account information, chat content, and outputs: while your account is active. We delete or anonymize them within 12 months after your account closes, or sooner if you ask.
  • Billing and tax records: as long as the law requires.
  • Your privacy choice: in your browser until you clear it.

If information sits in backups and cannot be deleted right away, we keep it isolated until it can be.
