08 — QUESTIONS

What people ask before their first pack.

What exactly do I get from one URL?

One researched content pack: a cited business profile, competitors, buyer questions, keyword targets and content gaps; a 1,200–1,800-word article in Markdown and HTML; a search title, meta description, direct answers, 8 FAQs, JSON-LD schema, internal link suggestions and a featured-image prompt with alt text; and a week of social posts plus a short-video script.

Will it know my business, or is it generic AI filler?

It starts from your website and uses only the business facts it can support there, each with its source link, plus anything you tell it. Anything it cannot verify is marked for your confirmation.

How does it avoid making things up?

Statistics stay only when a source is attached; if a number cannot be sourced, it is removed. Facts it cannot verify are flagged with [confirm] instead of guessed. It never invents testimonials, results, or customer names.

Does it post to my website or social accounts?

No. It creates drafts in copy-ready blocks. You review, then paste them into your website editor and social tools.

What does it cost?

The Growth plan is $97 per month for up to 8 content packs a month, about two a week. Your first full pack is free, with no card needed to try it. Cancel anytime.

Does it guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No. It produces researched, well-structured content built to be easy for search engines and AI answer engines to understand and quote. Results depend on your site, your market, and what you publish.

What if my website has very little on it?

It works with what it can verify and flags missing or uncertain details for your review, so you can fill them in before publishing.

Can I change what it writes?

Yes. Tell the Editor what to change — a shorter post, a warmer intro, a different call to action — and it changes that part only.
