Clear expectations

What it does—and where you lead.

Does this create actual ads or just concepts?

It produces finished static ad image files plus copy, hypotheses, evidence, and a handoff package. These are campaign-ready creative drafts for review; your team still checks the facts, approves the work, and handles platform setup and publication.

Does it guarantee high conversions?

No tool can responsibly promise that from creative files alone. The ADS gives you distinct hypotheses to test. Performance depends on audience, offer, landing page, media setup, budget, and the market.

Does it publish or spend my ad budget?

No. The current workflow prepares and hands off campaign files. It does not publish ads or place media spend. You decide if and when to launch.

Can it make UGC videos for any business?

The general deliverable described here is static creative. A dedicated UGC workflow has been tested for the pilot asset, but broad self-serve video production for arbitrary businesses is not yet established as a standard deliverable.

What evidence goes into the ads?

The campaign kit includes sourced evidence and hypotheses associated with the creative direction. Review sources and verify product claims before approving the final ads.
