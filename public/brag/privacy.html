Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026. Applies to Brag at conversionworks.site/brag.

This policy explains what personal information Brag collects, why, who helps us process it, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the Brag pages at conversionworks.site/brag and to the Brag chat. Our company-wide notice is at ministryai.ai/privacy-policy. Where the two differ for this product, this policy applies.

The short version

  • You give us a website address and what you type in the chat. We use it to plan and make your video.
  • The chat loads only after you allow it. No advertising cookies. No cross-site tracking.
  • Finished videos stay on our render service for 7 days so you can download them. Then they are deleted.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising, and we do not use your content to train AI models.
  • You can ask to see, correct, or delete your information at any time: support@ministryai.ai.

1. Who we are

Brag is provided by Ministry Dynamics LLC, doing business as MinistryAI ("we," "us," "our"), 777 Loma Vista Dr, Redding, CA 96002, United States. For the personal information described here, we are the controller: we decide why and how it is processed.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Website addresses and chat messages. The URLs you submit and anything you type in the chat, such as launch details, dates, and changes you ask for.
  • Account and billing information. If the chat asks you to sign in or pay, accounts are managed by our chat provider, MeclabsAI, and payments by Stripe. We receive your name, email address, payment status, and amount. We never receive your full card number.
  • Messages to us. What you send when you email us.

Information created or collected when you use Brag

  • Content the service reads. To plan your video, Brag reads public web pages, including your site. It copies the text, colors, fonts, logo, and images it needs into the video. Those pages can contain names or photos their owners published.
  • Your outputs. The plan, storyboard, video, poster image, and share copy Brag creates for you.
  • Usage and device data. If you allow the chat, it records how it is used and technical data such as IP address, browser type, and the page where the chat opened. Our website host and render service keep standard server logs.
  • Your privacy choice. Whether you allowed the chat, stored in your own browser.

We do not ask for sensitive personal information. Please do not enter health details, government ID numbers, or similar information in the chat.

6. How the AI works with your data

The chat assistant reads what you type and the public pages you point it to, then writes a plan and storyboard. When you approve it, the render service gives your storyboard and site address to Claude, which writes the video as a web page, records it, adds music, and checks every frame for spelling. Both providers state in their business terms that they do not train their models on this data by default, and we do not use your content to train AI models. Website text is treated as material for the video, never as instructions.

7. International transfers

Our providers process data in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. How long we keep it

  • Finished videos and render files: 7 days, then deleted automatically. Download your video within that time.
  • Chat conversations and accounts: kept by MeclabsAI while your account is active, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Billing records: as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Server logs: for our hosting providers' standard periods, or longer if needed to investigate abuse.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, and providers with strong security programs. The render service holds no payment data and runs each video in its own folder. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not share information you need to keep secret.
